Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy explains what Keyplan LLC ("Keyplan", "we") collects through this website, keyplan.dev, and through the EvoArc app, and how we use it.

This website

  • The site has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Like any website, the company that hosts ours keeps standard server logs: your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. These logs are used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts and other files are served from keyplan.dev, not from third-party services.

When you contact us

If you email or call us, we receive your email address or phone number and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Our email is provided by Google Workspace.

The EvoArc app

EvoArc is a field app for people who work on existing buildings: plans, photos, inspections and project notes. Accounts are set up by us or by the company you work with; you can't create one from the app. Demo mode shows sample data built from public records and needs no account.

Your account

  • Your name, work email address and password, used to sign you in. The app keeps a sign-in session on your phone so you don't have to sign in every time.
  • A record that you accepted our Terms of Use, with the date, time and version.

Location

  • If you allow location access, the app uses your phone's precise location to show the projects and buildings near you. That matching happens on your phone, against a list of buildings the app has downloaded; your live location is not sent to our server for it.
  • Photos you upload keep the location stored in the photo (its GPS position). When you take a photo in the app, the app adds that position to the photo. The location goes to our server with the photo, so the photo can be filed with the right building.
  • The app doesn't track your location in the background.

Photos, plans and project work

  • Photos you choose or take, with their date, location and any notes you add.
  • Plans, plan markups, field reports, inspection marks, notes, tasks, time entries and contacts you add, including the image of a business card you scan.
  • This work is stored on servers we operate. Project files and photos are also kept in our file storage service, Egnyte. Text you type for time entries, and business card images, are read by software running on our own servers, not by an outside AI service.
  • When you scan a business card, our server may visit the website printed on the card to check the company name.

How the app is used, and crashes

  • The app sends our server a log of which screens and menus you open and for how long, and errors it runs into. It doesn't record what you type, captions, field values or search words. We use it to fix problems and improve the app.
  • If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. A report holds the error and stack trace, app version, phone model, Android version and free memory, plus the names of the last screens you opened. It doesn't include your name, email or project content. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days.
  • The app doesn't use Firebase Analytics or any advertising service, and it doesn't collect the Advertising ID.
  • If you're an administrator and allow notifications, your phone's Firebase Cloud Messaging token is stored on our server so we can send you system alerts.

Maps

Map backgrounds are loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which receive your IP address and the map area being shown.

Security

The app talks to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS), through Cloudflare, which protects our servers and checks that requests come from the app. Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage.

Sharing

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers that run our services: Cloudflare (network security), Egnyte (file storage), Google (email, push notifications and crash reports) and our website host. We also share it when the law requires it. Project work you add in EvoArc is visible to the people on that project at the company you work with.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep correspondence for as long as it's useful for supporting you or running the business. EvoArc account information is kept while your account is open. To delete your EvoArc account, see Delete your EvoArc account; it explains what is deleted and what is kept. You can also ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@keyplan.dev.

Children

This website and the EvoArc app aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date.
