Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what Keyplan LLC ("Keyplan", "we") collects through this website, keyplan.dev, and how we use it. We collect very little.

This website

  • The site has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Like any website, the company that hosts ours keeps standard server logs: your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. These logs are used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts and other files are served from keyplan.dev, not from third-party services.

When you contact us

If you email or call us, we receive your email address or phone number and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Our email is provided by Google Workspace.

Our apps

The Keyplan app hasn't been released. Before it is available on the App Store or Google Play, we'll update this policy to describe exactly what the app collects and why.

Sharing

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run our website and email, and when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep correspondence for as long as it's useful for supporting you or running the business. You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@keyplan.dev.

Children

This website isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date.
