Support

Get in touch

Email is the best way to reach us: support@keyplan.dev. You can also call (541) 236-2435 and leave a message.

What to include

  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead
  • Your device and its operating system version
  • The app version, if you're writing about the app
  • A screenshot, if it helps

Don't send drawings or documents you aren't allowed to share. If we need a file to track down a problem, we'll ask for it.

The Keyplan app

The app is still in development and isn't on the App Store or Google Play yet. Questions about it are welcome at the same address.

Privacy requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email support@keyplan.dev. See the privacy policy for details.
