AThe Keyplan app

The drawing set for a real building can run to hundreds of large sheets, and the part you need is usually a small piece of one of them.

Keyplan is a plan viewer that slices big sheets into the areas and details you're working on, so the right piece of the building is easy to find and easy to read on a phone or tablet.

It's in development now for iPhone, iPad and Android, and will be available on the App Store and Google Play. To hear when it's ready, email support@keyplan.dev.

BConsulting

We take on custom software work for architecture firms and other building-industry teams: tools for drawings and documents, building data, and the day-to-day workflows around them.

The company is run by a building enclosure consultant, so the software comes from time spent on real buildings with real drawing sets.
