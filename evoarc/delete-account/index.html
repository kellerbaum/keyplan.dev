Delete your EvoArc account

EvoArc app by Keyplan LLC · Updated November 21, 2026

You can ask us to delete your EvoArc account and the personal information connected to it at any time.

How to ask

  • By email: write to support@keyplan.dev from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my EvoArc account".
  • In the app: open the account menu (your initials, top right) and choose Delete account. This sends us the same request.

We'll confirm the request from your sign-in address, turn off sign-in for the account within 2 business days, and finish deleting within 30 days. If your account was set up by the company you work with, we'll let them know it has been closed.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in: name, email address and password, and any sign-in sessions.
  • Your app usage log (screens opened and errors).
  • Push notification tokens for your devices.
  • Your profile details and settings.

Data stored in the app on your phone is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Project work you added, such as photos, field reports, inspection marks, plan markups, notes and time entries, is part of the project record of the company you worked with. Deleting your account doesn't delete that record. It is kept for as long as the company keeps its project records, and items may still show that you created them. To ask for specific items to be removed, say which ones in your email.
  • Your Terms of Use acceptance record is kept for 7 years after your account is closed, as a legal record.
  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics don't contain your name or email; Google deletes them after 90 days.
  • Backups: copies of deleted information in server backups are removed as those backups are replaced, within 90 days.
  • We keep information longer if the law requires it.
